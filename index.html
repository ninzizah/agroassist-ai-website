Features

Everything Farmers Need in One App

From AI-powered diagnosis to expert consultations, AgroAssist AI is a complete agricultural intelligence platform.

AI Crop Disease Diagnosis

Take a photo of any diseased crop and get instant diagnosis with disease name, severity percentage, and confidence score. Our AI identifies 50+ diseases across tomato, potato, maize, rice, coffee, and more.

Core Feature

Rwanda-Approved Medicine

Only recommends RICA/RAB-registered agrochemicals with exact dosage, active ingredients, and safety class.

Works Offline

Scans queue locally and sync when internet returns. Built for Rwanda's rural connectivity challenges.

English & Kinyarwanda

Full bilingual support with one-tap language toggle. Every screen, every message in your language.

Expert Consultations

Connect directly with certified agronomists in your district for personalized advice on complex cases.

Weather Intelligence

GPS-based weather data with spray advisory (safe/unsafe to spray) and 3-day forecast for your location.

Market Prices

AI-powered real-time crop market prices for Rwanda. Know what your harvest is worth before you sell.

PDF Reports

Export branded diagnostic certificates with full disease analysis, treatment plan, and expert recommendations.

Voice AI Consultant

Live voice conversation with an AI agronomist. Designed for accessibility — works even for visually impaired farmers.

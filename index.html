The Problem

Rwandan Farmers Lose Crops Every Season

Smallholder farmers produce 80% of Rwanda's food, but face critical challenges that threaten their livelihoods.

Late Detection

Farmers often discover crop diseases too late, after significant damage has already occurred. Without early warning systems, entire harvests can be lost.

No Expert Access

Rwanda has only ~200 agronomists for 2+ million farmers. Most smallholders cannot reach a qualified expert when their crops get sick.

Wrong Treatments

Farmers rely on neighbor advice or shop recommendations, often buying wrong or banned pesticides that waste money and harm the environment.

Language Barriers

Most agricultural apps are in English only. Rwanda's farmers need solutions in Kinyarwanda to be truly accessible and effective.
